# TRACE-X

## Blockchain Forensic Intelligence & Cryptocurrency Investigation Platform

TRACE-X is a full-stack blockchain intelligence and cryptocurrency forensic investigation platform designed to transform suspicious cryptocurrency wallet activity into structured, explainable investigation intelligence.

TRACE-X combines wallet analysis, transaction analysis, behavioral analytics, fraud-pattern detection, deterministic risk scoring, interactive blockchain graphs, multi-hop fund tracing, money-flow analysis, wallet clustering, entity and VASP intelligence, cross-chain intelligence, attribution evidence, AI-assisted investigation, monitoring, dataset ingestion, professional reporting, and controlled incident simulation into one unified platform.

> **Demo / Offline Mode:** TRACE-X is designed to operate without external blockchain or AI API keys by using deterministic demo providers and a local intelligence fallback.

---

# 🚀 Product Vision

TRACE-X follows one core principle:

> **Follow the money. Reconstruct the story. Explain the evidence.**

A suspicious wallet should not be treated as an isolated blockchain address.

TRACE-X progressively analyzes:

- Transactions
- Wallet behavior
- Connected wallets
- Fund movement
- Fraud patterns
- Risk
- Graph relationships
- Multi-hop paths
- Wallet clusters
- Potential services and entities
- Cross-chain relationships
- Attribution evidence
- Investigation timelines
- AI-generated intelligence
- Professional forensic reports

TRACE-X helps investigators understand:

- Where funds came from
- Where funds went
- How quickly funds moved
- Which wallets interacted with the subject wallet
- Whether suspicious behavioral patterns exist
- Whether funds are being forwarded or layered
- Whether fan-in or fan-out patterns exist
- Which wallets appear behaviorally connected
- Whether potential entity or service exposure exists
- Whether potential cross-chain movement exists
- What evidence supports each finding
- How confident each conclusion is
- What remains unknown

# 📋 Development Principles

## 1. Follow the Money

Make fund movement easy to understand.

## 2. Explain Every Risk

Risk scores must expose their contributors.

## 3. Connect Evidence

Findings should link to transactions, wallets, graphs and timelines.

## 4. Separate Facts from Inference

Observed facts must be distinguishable from analytical conclusions.

## 5. Protect Access

Backend authorization is the final security authority.

## 6. Preserve Provenance

Show whether information came from blockchain data, uploaded datasets or simulation.

## 7. Maintain Data Consistency

All modules must use a shared investigation model.

## 8. Avoid Unsupported Attribution

Do not claim real-world identity or ownership without sufficient evidence.

## 9. Keep Simulation Controlled

The simulator is hypothetical and does not perform real attacks.

## 10. No Dead Buttons

If a feature appears in the UI, it should perform a real application action.
